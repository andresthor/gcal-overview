# Privacy Policy

gcal-overview requests read-only access to your Google Calendar events via the Google Calendar API (scope: `calendar.events.readonly`).

- **What we access:** your calendar event titles, start/end times, and event links.
- **How we use it:** displayed locally in a terminal dashboard on your own machine.
- **Storage:** no data is stored on any server. Events are read live each poll and not persisted beyond your local machine.
- **Sharing:** no data is shared with any third party.

This app is for personal use and is not verified by Google.
